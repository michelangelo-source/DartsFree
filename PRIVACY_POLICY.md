# Privacy Policy for DartsFree

**Effective Date:** 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DartsFree ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use the DartsFree mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

DartsFree is designed to operate locally on your device. All data related to the App, including but not limited to:
- Player names
- Match scores
- Tournament brackets
- Game history and statistics
- Custom application settings

...are stored securely and exclusively on your local device storage. We do not transmit any of this data to external servers, nor do we have access to it.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Tracking

The App is ad-free and does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks that would monitor your usage behavior or collect personal information.

## 3. Permissions Required

The App requests only the permissions strictly necessary for its core functionality to operate optimally on your device. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data, it inherently complies with children's privacy protection laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The App is safe for users of all ages.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are added that change our data practices. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via our GitHub repository issues page.
